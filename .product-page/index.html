Start with installation ↗
01 → 02 → 03Import. Configure. Choose.
  1. 01

    Bring your test accounts.

    Open Settings, name a CSV group, and choose your file. Include account_id, username, and password; extra columns become searchable metadata. Set the authorized domain, verify the file, then confirm the import.

    Start with “Download sample CSV (3 rows)” in Settings.
  2. 02

    Tell it where to help.

    Configure the group’s login profile: your authorized login domain, username and password matching rules, and a success URL or visible element. Save and approve the site permission.

    Importing accounts alone doesn’t authorize autofill.
  3. 03

    Find. Fill. You take it from here.

    Visit the authorized login page and open the extension. Choose a group, search by ID, username, or metadata, and select an account. Review it, choose Autofill, then submit the website’s form yourself.

    “Recent” means filled. “Last successful” means verified.

DEVELOPER INSTALLATION · NOT A WEB STORE RELEASE

Up and running in four steps.

  1. 1

    Get the extension package. Extract account-login-assistant.zip from your project or team maintainer. If you build from source, use the generated dist folder.

  2. 2

    Open Chrome’s extensions page. Enter chrome://extensions in the address bar and switch on Developer mode.

  3. 3

    Load your extension. Select Load unpacked and choose the extracted dist folder. Keep this folder in place.

  4. 4

    Pin it. Make it yours. Pin Login Assistant from Chrome’s Extensions menu, then open Settings to import your first group.

A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Good to know.
Before you go.

Will it work with every login page?+

It supports top-level, visible, writable username and password inputs. Embedded frames, closed shadow roots, SSO handoffs, and multi-step logins are outside this version’s scope. Configure and test your own site’s field matching and success signal before relying on it.

Can I use it on localhost?+

Yes. HTTPS sites are supported, as is plain HTTP on localhost, *.localhost, and 127.0.0.1. Authorization matches the exact origin, including its port. Subdomains need separate authorization; a path does not narrow autofill access.

What’s the difference between Merge and Replace?+

Merge & Update adds or updates accounts by ID and keeps omitted accounts in the selected group. Wipe & Replace replaces that group’s entire dataset. Invalid rows are excluded, so review the verification results carefully before confirming Replace. Other groups are unaffected.

How do recent and successful logins work?+

Recent records a successful field fill. Last successful changes only when the configured success condition is observed within two minutes. A failed login leaves the previous successful account unchanged. Editing filled values cancels attribution.

What should I know about CSV files?+

Imports support UTF-8 CSV files up to 2 MiB, 10,000 rows, and 100 columns. Account IDs must be unique within a group. Export CSV downloads the entire selected group, including synthetic passwords. Original and exported CSV files remain on your disk; the extension does not encrypt or delete them.
